Featured · Sensor fusion · 2026

Dark Track

My capstone. It fuses live ADS-B flight data from the OpenSky Network with a simulated radar into one tracked picture, using Kalman filters and Hungarian-algorithm association, then flags aircraft whose transponder goes quiet while radar keeps tracking them. On held-out data it catches 93–98% of those dropouts with zero false alarms, at about 500 tracks in under half a second.

PythonNumPy / SciPyPostgreSQLKalman filterLeaflet

Featured · iOS app · 2026

Dark Wake

Dark Track at sea. An iPad and iPhone app that fuses live AIS ship transponders with a simulated coastal radar, then flags ships that switch their AIS off while radar still tracks them. On a real iPad over the Singapore Strait it caught an injected dropout within about a minute; in simulation it catches 87 of 88 with zero false alarms.
